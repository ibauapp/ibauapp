# Hi there, we're iBau 👋 🐾

Welcome to the official GitHub profile for **iBau** – the all-in-one digital pet care ecosystem designed for dog owners, expats, and pet care businesses in Italy.

---

### 🐶 What is iBau?

[iBau](https://ibau.app) connects dog owners with verified local dog daycares, pet boarding facilities, groomers, and professional trainers across Italy. Our platform helps pet parents streamline their dog's daily routines and medical records seamlessly.

* 🏥 **Bau File:** A digital health book to manage vaccinations, medical histories, and reminders.
* 🏡 **Verified Dog Daycares:** Find and book trusted [asili canini e pensioni per cani in Italia](https://ibau.app).
* 📖 **Dog Breed Guides:** Explore detailed profiles, care guides, and health insights in our [guida alle razze canine](https://ibau.app/razze-cani).
